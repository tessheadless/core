Tess vs Hygraph

GraphQL-native headless CMS (SaaS)

A hosted, GraphQL-native CMS (formerly GraphCMS), known for content federation across remote APIs.

Side by side

Tess compared with Hygraph
CriterionTessHygraph
DeliverySoftware you run yourselfManaged SaaS only
APIGraphQL generated from the modelsGraphQL generated from the models, with content federation of remote sources
Content modelsAdmin UI or API, applied immediatelySchema editor in the web app, with environments
Data locationYour servers and your databaseHygraph's cloud, in the regions it offers
Review workflowPer content type, in every editionContent stages and workflows, depending on the plan
LocalizationNo per-field content localization yetBuilt-in localization
ExtensibilitySandboxed WebAssembly pluginsUI extensions, webhooks and remote sources
PricingCommunity free for personal use; Enterprise license for commercial useFree tier, then paid plans by usage and features

Choose Hygraph if…

  • You don't want to operate any infrastructure.
  • You need content federation or built-in localization.

Choose Tess if…

  • Your content must stay on your own infrastructure or database.
  • You want the same GraphQL-first experience without usage-based pricing.

Moving from Hygraph to Tess

Hygraph's schema translates directly: models become content types, components become components, and union/modular fields become DYNAMIC_ZONE fields. Describe them in the tess-transfer YAML format, import them, then copy the entries with a GraphQL-to-GraphQL script.

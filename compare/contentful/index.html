Tess vs Contentful

Composable content platform (SaaS)

An enterprise headless CMS delivered as SaaS, with REST and GraphQL delivery APIs and a large app ecosystem.

Side by side

Tess compared with Contentful
CriterionTessContentful
DeliverySoftware you run yourselfManaged SaaS only
APIGraphQL generated from the modelsREST delivery and management APIs, plus a GraphQL Content API
Content modelsAdmin UI or API, applied immediatelyWeb app or management API, with environments
Data locationYour servers and your databaseContentful's cloud
LocalizationNo per-field content localization yetBuilt-in locales
ExtensibilitySandboxed WebAssembly pluginsApp Framework and marketplace
PricingCommunity free for personal use; Enterprise license for commercial useFree tier, then paid plans with usage limits

Choose Contentful if…

  • You need an enterprise SaaS with SLAs, many locales and a large app marketplace.

Choose Tess if…

  • You want to own the infrastructure and the data.
  • You prefer a predictable license to usage-based limits.

Moving from Contentful to Tess

Contentful content types and their fields map to Tess content types; references become RELATION fields and assets become Asset relations. Import the models with tess-transfer, then copy entries with the Contentful export and the Tess GraphQL API.

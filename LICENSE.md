`SPDX-License-Identifier: LicenseRef-Tess-Community-Enterprise`, which refers
to this license.

Tess is distributed under a **hybrid license** with two editions:

## Community edition — personal use

The Community edition may be used, studied and modified for **personal,
non-commercial use**.

## Enterprise edition — commercial use

Any **commercial use** of Tess (use by or for a business, inside a commercial
product or service, or to provide services to third parties) requires an
**Enterprise** license. The Enterprise edition includes advanced features and
plugins maintained by the Tess Dev Team, and hosting solutions for Tess.

## To be defined by the final license

- Exact definitions of "personal use" and "commercial use".
- Whether modified versions may be redistributed, and under which terms.
- The license of the plugin and client **examples** in `examples/` (a
  permissive license would let anyone build plugins and clients freely).
- Third-party plugins: ownership and licensing of plugins written by users.
- Contribution terms (e.g. a contributor license agreement).
- Contact for Enterprise licensing (website: <https://tessheadless.com>).

Third-party dependencies keep their own licenses.
